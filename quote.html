Sector Systems

For larger or higher-security perimeter installations, sectorisation can provide significantly better visibility of where a perimeter event is occurring.

A properly designed sector system allows the perimeter to be divided into defined areas so that security personnel can identify the affected zone rather than simply receiving a general perimeter alarm.
