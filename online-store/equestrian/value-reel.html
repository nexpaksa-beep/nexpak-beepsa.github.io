About the Nylon Value Reel

The JVA Nylon Value Reel is designed for portable electric fencing applications where fencing tape or polywire needs to be deployed, tensioned and recovered efficiently.

Its heavy-duty nylon construction and galvanised steel frame make it suitable for demanding agricultural and equestrian environments.

For horse fencing, a reel can be particularly useful when creating temporary paddocks, strip-grazing areas or movable boundaries.

Correct Use & Handling

1

Switch Off the Energiser

Always switch off and isolate the energiser before connecting, disconnecting or handling the electric fence conductor.

2

Position the Reel

Place or mount the reel securely so it cannot move unexpectedly while the conductor is being deployed.

3

Connect the Conductor

Attach the appropriate conductor to the reel and ensure the electrical connection is secure before operation.

4

Deploy the Fence

Unwind the tape or polywire smoothly while maintaining appropriate tension and avoiding twists or tangles.

5

Secure the Fence

Once the fence is positioned, secure the reel and conductor so the system remains stable during normal use.
