Flexible Gate Solutions for Horse Paddocks

A 50-metre bulk roll of electric bungy cord allows installers and property owners to create multiple custom gate breaks without purchasing pre-cut gate sections. Its flexible, elastic construction allows the cord to give when a gate is opened or closed, making it particularly useful for equestrian fencing applications where visibility, flexibility and careful installation are important.

Performance

Key Performance Factors

Visibility & Safety

A clearly visible gate conductor helps horses, riders, handlers and visitors identify the boundary and gate opening. High-visibility materials are particularly useful around paddocks and frequently used gateways.

Elastic Flexibility

Bungy cord can stretch when a gate is opened and return towards its original length when released. Correct tension is important to prevent the conductor from sagging onto vegetation or the ground.

Outdoor Construction

These products are designed for outdoor electric fencing environments where exposure to sunlight, moisture and regular handling can occur.

Important Installation Note

Do not size a bungy gate simply by cutting the cord to exactly the same length as the physical gate opening. The correct relaxed length depends on the product, gate configuration, attachment hardware and required operating tension.

The conductor should remain safely suspended when installed and should not be allowed to contact grass, vegetation, soil or other conductive surfaces.

Always follow the manufacturer's installation instructions and applicable electric-fence safety requirements.

Installation Principle

Why the Cord Should Not Simply Match the Opening

Too Much Slack

If the conductor is installed with excessive slack, it can sag and potentially contact grass, vegetation or the ground.

Correct Operating Tension

The gate should have enough tension to remain safely elevated while still allowing the gate to be opened and closed without placing excessive strain on the conductor or fittings.

Hardware Matters

Gate handles, hooks, springs and connection points can all affect the final required conductor length.

Complete Gate System

What Else Do You Need?

Bungy cord is the conductor. A complete gate requires compatible hardware and a properly connected electric fence circuit.

Insulated Gate Handle

Provides a safe insulated grip for opening and closing the electric fence gate.

Gate Hooks / Anchors

Suitable attachment hardware is required to secure the flexible conductor at the gate posts.

Fence Connections

The gate conductor must form part of the electric fence circuit using suitable electrical connections.

Under-Gate Connection

Where required, an appropriately installed underground or protected connection can maintain continuity to the next section of fencing when the gate is open.

Safety

Important Electric Fence Safety

Electric fencing must be designed, installed and maintained in accordance with applicable regulations and the manufacturer's instructions.

  • Keep the electric fence clearly identifiable.
  • Keep conductors clear of vegetation and conductive surfaces.
  • Use suitable insulated gate hardware.
  • Inspect the gate regularly for damaged conductors, fittings or connections.
  • Never improvise electrical connections with unsuitable materials.
  • Follow the applicable South African electric-fence regulations and standards.
Maintenance

Keep Your Bungy Gate Working Properly

Regular inspection helps identify mechanical and electrical problems before they become a safety or reliability issue.

01

Inspect the Cord

02

Check the Fittings

03

Check Clearance

04

Check Electrical Continuity

Before You Install

Bungy Gate Installation Checklist

Use this checklist when planning a flexible electric fence gate for a horse paddock.

Measure the gate opening

Confirm the distance between the two attachment points before determining the required bungy cord length.

Select compatible gate hardware

Use suitable insulated handles, hooks, connectors and other components designed for electric fence applications.

Allow for operating tension

The conductor should remain elevated while still allowing the gate to open and close smoothly.

Check vegetation clearance

Keep grass, weeds, branches and other vegetation away from the energized conductor.

Test the completed fence

After installation, check the fence using appropriate electric-fence testing equipment.

Inspect regularly

Flexible gate conductors experience repeated movement and should be inspected regularly for wear or damage.

Avoid These Problems

Common Bungy Gate Mistakes

Cutting the cord too long

Excessive slack can allow the conductor to sag and potentially contact vegetation or the ground.

Using unsuitable connectors

Improvised or incompatible connections can create unreliable electrical and mechanical connections.

Ignoring vegetation

Vegetation touching an electric fence can cause leakage and reduce the effectiveness of the fence system.

Forgetting gate continuity

Where the fence layout requires it, ensure the appropriate connection arrangement maintains electrical continuity to the next section.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bungy Cord Questions

What is electrified bungy cord?

Electrified bungy cord is a flexible electric fence conductor designed for applications where the conductor needs to move or stretch, such as paddock and driveway gates.

How long is the roll?

The products covered on this page are supplied as 50-metre bulk rolls. Always confirm the exact product specification before ordering.

Can I use it for horse paddocks?

Flexible electric-fence conductors can be used in suitable equestrian gate applications when installed correctly and in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and applicable safety requirements.

How many gates can one 50m roll make?

The exact number depends on the width of each opening and the required installation length. As a planning example, approximately 13–14 four-metre gates may be possible when using around 3.5–3.7 metres of relaxed cord per gate. Actual yield will vary.

Do I need a gate handle?

A suitable insulated gate handle is normally required where the gate is intended to be opened and closed by hand. Use hardware that is compatible with the selected conductor and installation.
