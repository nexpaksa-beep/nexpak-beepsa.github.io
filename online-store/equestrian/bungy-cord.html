50m Electric Bungy Cord

Flexible electrified bungy cord, also known as elastic shock cord or flexi rope, provides a practical solution for creating custom electric fence gate openings in horse paddocks.

Flexible Gate Solutions for Horse Paddocks

A 50-metre bulk roll of electric bungy cord allows installers and property owners to create multiple custom gate breaks without purchasing pre-cut gate sections. Its flexible, elastic construction allows the cord to give when a gate is opened or closed, making it particularly useful for equestrian fencing applications where visibility, flexibility and careful installation are important.

Product Overview

Why Use Electric Bungy Cord?

Flexible gate conductors can be configured around different paddock layouts and gate openings.

Flexible Gate Opening

The elastic construction allows the conductor to stretch and retract, making it suitable for temporary or frequently opened paddock gateways.

50m Bulk Roll

A bulk roll provides enough material for multiple gate sections, allowing lengths to be selected according to individual gate openings.

High Visibility

High-visibility fencing conductors help make paddock boundaries and gate openings easier for horses and handlers to see.
