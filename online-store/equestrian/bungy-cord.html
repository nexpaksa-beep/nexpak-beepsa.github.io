Flexible Gate Solutions for Horse Paddocks

A 50-metre bulk roll of electric bungy cord allows installers and property owners to create multiple custom gate breaks without purchasing pre-cut gate sections. Its flexible, elastic construction allows the cord to give when a gate is opened or closed, making it particularly useful for equestrian fencing applications where visibility, flexibility and careful installation are important.

Product Overview

Why Use Electric Bungy Cord?

Flexible gate conductors can be configured around different paddock layouts and gate openings.

Flexible Gate Opening

The elastic construction allows the conductor to stretch and retract, making it suitable for temporary or frequently opened paddock gateways.

50m Bulk Roll

A bulk roll provides enough material for multiple gate sections, allowing lengths to be selected according to individual gate openings.

High Visibility

High-visibility fencing conductors help make paddock boundaries and gate openings easier for horses and handlers to see.

Performance

Key Performance Factors

Visibility & Safety

A clearly visible gate conductor helps horses, riders, handlers and visitors identify the boundary and gate opening. High-visibility materials are particularly useful around paddocks and frequently used gateways.

Elastic Flexibility

Bungy cord can stretch when a gate is opened and return towards its original length when released. Correct tension is important to prevent the conductor from sagging onto vegetation or the ground.

Outdoor Construction

These products are designed for outdoor electric fencing environments where exposure to sunlight, moisture and regular handling can occur.

Important Installation Note

Do not size a bungy gate simply by cutting the cord to exactly the same length as the physical gate opening. The correct relaxed length depends on the product, gate configuration, attachment hardware and required operating tension.

The conductor should remain safely suspended when installed and should not be allowed to contact grass, vegetation, soil or other conductive surfaces.

Always follow the manufacturer's installation instructions and applicable electric-fence safety requirements.

Practical Application

Using Bungy Cord for Paddock Gates

A typical flexible gate system consists of the bungy conductor, insulated gate hardware and suitable fence connections.

01

Measure the Opening

Measure the actual distance between the two gate attachment points before cutting the conductor.

02

Determine the Required Length

Allow for the tension and operating requirements of the selected gate hardware. Do not assume every installation requires the same cut length.

03

Install the Gate Hardware

Connect the bungy conductor using compatible insulated gate handles, hooks and connection hardware.

04

Check the Finished Gate

Check that the conductor remains elevated, visible and mechanically secure throughout the opening and closing cycle.
