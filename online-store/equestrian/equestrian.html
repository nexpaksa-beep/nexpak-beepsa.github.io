PADDOCK FENCE GUIDE

How to build a horse paddock fence

A good horse fence starts with visibility, correct tension and a properly powered energizer. Use components designed to work together.

1
Plan your paddock

Measure the perimeter, decide gate positions and choose the number of fence strands.

2
Choose visible conductors

Horse tape or suitable polyrope gives the fence a clear visual boundary.

3
Install insulators

Use intermediate, corner and strain insulators appropriate to your posts and tape.

4
Install the energizer

Select a JVA energizer suited to the fence length and grounding arrangement.

5
Finish gates and warning signs

Use gate breaks, clips and warning signs to create a practical finished installation.

Fence design should be appropriate for the horses, terrain and local requirements. For a permanent installation, have the system checked by a competent installer.
