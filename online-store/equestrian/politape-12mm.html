About 12mm Poly Tape

12mm poly tape is commonly used in equestrian electric fencing where a lightweight and highly visible conductor is required.

It is particularly useful for temporary horse paddocks, strip grazing and portable fencing. It can also be used as a secondary lower strand beneath a wider 40mm horse tape.

The tape consists of UV-stabilized polyethylene woven together with conductive metal strands. These conductors carry the electrical pulse from the energiser through the fence.

Why visibility matters Horses can see a wide, contrasting fence conductor more easily than a thin wire. Good visibility can help horses recognise the fence boundary and reduce accidental contact.

Electrical Performance

The resistance of electric fence tape determines how easily the electrical pulse travels along the conductor.

Stainless Steel Conductors Durable and corrosion resistant, making them suitable for outdoor fencing applications.
Mixed-Metal Conductors Some premium tapes combine stainless steel with highly conductive metals to reduce resistance.
Longer Fence Runs Low-resistance tape is generally better suited to longer installations.
Short Portable Runs Standard stainless-steel tape can be practical for shorter temporary paddocks.
