SAFETY FIRST

Make the presence of an electric fence clear

Warning signs provide an immediate visual indication that a fence is electrified. They are an important part of responsible electric-fence installation, particularly where people, visitors, workers or members of the public could come into contact with the fence.

For equestrian properties, clear signage is especially useful around paddocks, access gates, stable areas, training yards and fence lines bordering driveways or public access areas.

Clearly visible. Securely mounted. Easy to recognise. Warning signage should be positioned so that it remains clearly visible and securely attached to the fence or an appropriate supporting structure.
