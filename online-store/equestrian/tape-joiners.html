Electric Fence Tape Joiners

What Is an Electric Fence Tape Joiner?

An electric fence tape joiner is a specialised connection component used to join two sections of conductive poly tape while maintaining an effective electrical path between them.

Electric fence tape contains conductive strands woven through the polymer tape. A normal knot can damage, twist or break these strands and may create a poor electrical connection.

A proper tape joiner is designed to provide a controlled mechanical connection while allowing the conductive elements in the two sections of tape to make reliable electrical contact.

What You Will Learn

  • How tape joiners work
  • Always use a purpose-designed electric fence tape joiner rather than tying the tape in a knot.
  • Make sure both tape ends are clean, flat and free from excessive dirt, corrosion or damaged material before joining them.
  • The join must maintain both the mechanical strength of the fence and a reliable electrical connection between the conductive strands.
  • After making the connection, inspect the join for sharp edges, exposed metal or loose material that could present a risk to horses or handlers.
TRAINING GUIDE

How to Join Electric Fence Tape Correctly

A good tape join is more than simply connecting two pieces of tape. The connection must preserve the electrical path while keeping the fence mechanically secure.

01

Switch off the energizer

Before working on an electric fence, isolate the energizer and make sure the fence is no longer energized. Never work on a live fence.

02

Prepare the tape

Cut away badly damaged or frayed sections. The tape ends should be clean, straight and suitable for insertion into the joiner.

03

Select the correct joiner

Match the joiner to the tape width. A joiner designed for 40mm horse tape should not be used as a substitute for hardware designed for narrow 12mm tape.

04

Insert both tape ends

Feed the two tape ends through the joiner according to the manufacturer's design. Ensure the conductive strands make proper contact with the metal portion of the joiner.

05

Secure the connection

For clamp-style joiners, tighten the screws evenly so that the tape is held firmly without unnecessarily crushing or damaging it.

For weave-through buckle-style joiners, pull the tape firmly through the buckle so that the connection remains secure under normal fence tension.

06

Inspect the finished join

Check that the tape runs straight through the joiner and that there are no loose sections, exposed sharp edges or damaged conductors.

07

Restore power and test the fence

Once the work area is clear and the connection is secure, restore the energizer according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Use a suitable electric fence voltage tester to verify that the fence is receiving an appropriate voltage beyond the join.

TECHNICIAN TRAINING

Common Tape Joining Mistakes

Poor joining techniques can create both mechanical failures and electrical losses along the fence.

Tying a knot

Knots can damage the conductive strands inside the tape and create a poor electrical connection.

Wrong joiner size

Always use a joiner suitable for the width and construction of the tape being installed.

Over-tightening

Excessive clamping force can damage the tape, especially when using screw-type joiners.

Not testing voltage

A fence may look physically correct while still having poor electrical continuity. Test the fence after completing the repair.

Which Tape Joiner Do You Need?

40mm Horse Tape

For standard equestrian horse fencing, choose a joiner specifically designed for wide 40mm tape.

High-Wind Areas

A heavy-duty clamp-style joiner can provide a secure mechanical connection where the tape is exposed to significant movement.

Field Repairs

A simple weave-through joiner can be convenient when a fast repair is required without tools.

Electric Fence Safety

Always isolate the energizer before working on an electric fence. Follow the manufacturer's installation instructions and applicable local electric-fence regulations.

After completing a repair, inspect the fence and use an appropriate fence tester to verify that the system is operating correctly.

FAQ

Tape Joiner Questions

Can I simply tie electric fence tape together?

It is not recommended. A knot can damage the conductive strands and create an unreliable electrical connection. Use a purpose-designed tape joiner.

Can I use a 40mm joiner on 12mm tape?

No. Use a joiner designed for the specific tape width and construction being installed.

Why is my fence voltage low after joining the tape?

Check the join for poor contact, damaged conductive strands, corrosion, loose hardware or an incorrect joiner. Also inspect the rest of the fence for vegetation contact or other faults.

Should I test the fence after repairing a join?

Yes. After the fence has been safely re-energized, use a suitable fence voltage tester to confirm that the connection is electrically continuous.
