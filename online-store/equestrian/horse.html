Correct Use & Installation

Horse fence tape should be installed as part of a properly designed fencing system. The following provides general guidance.

1

Plan the Fence

Determine the paddock perimeter, fence height and number of fencing lines required for the property.

2

Position the Posts

Install suitable fencing posts and ensure they are secure and appropriate for the terrain.

3

Install Suitable Insulators

Use fencing insulators designed to accommodate the width and type of tape being installed.

4

Install the Tape

Feed the tape through the appropriate insulators and maintain a neat, consistent fence line.

5

Tension Correctly

Apply appropriate tension while avoiding excessive tension that could damage the tape or fittings.

6

Test the Fence

Test the completed electric fence using a suitable fence tester before introducing horses to the paddock.

Safety & Best Practice

Horse fencing should always be designed with animal safety as a priority. Electric fencing components must be correctly installed and maintained.

  • Use appropriate fencing components designed for the tape.
  • Keep the fence line clearly visible and properly maintained.
  • Check the fence regularly for damage, loose fittings or vegetation contact.
  • Use a suitable electric fence tester to check the system.
  • Follow the manufacturer's installation and safety instructions for the complete electric fencing system.
  • Ensure gates and access points are properly designed and safely managed.
