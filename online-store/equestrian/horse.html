About 40mm Horse Fence Tape

40mm electric horse fence tape, commonly referred to as polytape, is designed to provide a highly visible electric barrier for equestrian fencing applications.

The wide tape construction provides substantially more visual presence than narrow electric fence wire, making it particularly suitable for horse paddocks, arenas, temporary grazing areas and boundary fencing.

Important: Horse fencing is not simply about choosing a wide tape. The complete system should be properly designed using compatible tape, insulators, connectors, posts, earthing and an appropriate energiser.
📋

General Specifications

The following specifications represent common characteristics of 40mm electric horse fencing tape. Exact specifications can vary between manufacturers and product ranges.

Width 40mm (approximately 1.5 inches)
Standard Roll Length 200 metres. Some manufacturers also offer longer rolls such as 350 metres.
Material UV-stabilised polyethylene with integrated conductive elements.
Visibility Wide tape construction provides high visibility for equestrian applications.
Conductors Conductive metal strands are incorporated into the tape to carry the electrical pulse.
Applications Horse paddocks, equine boundaries, temporary fencing and grazing areas.
⚡

How the Tape Works

Electric fence tape combines a flexible polymer tape structure with conductive metal strands. When the tape is correctly connected to an energised electric fencing system, an electrical pulse travels through the conductive elements.

The tape therefore performs two important functions: it provides a highly visible physical boundary and it carries the electrical pulse throughout the fence circuit.

Visible Boundary

  • 40mm width provides strong visibility.
  • Suitable for horse paddocks and equestrian boundaries.
  • Helps make the fence visually obvious to animals and people.

Electrical Conductor

  • Integrated metal conductors carry the electrical pulse.
  • Connections must maintain good electrical contact.
  • Damaged or poorly connected tape can reduce system performance.
📊

Standard vs Low-Resistance Tape

Not all 40mm tape is electrically identical. Different products can use different numbers and types of conductive strands.

Specification Standard Poly Tape Heavy-Duty / Low Resistance
Conductors Commonly 6–10 stainless-steel strands May use stainless-steel and tinned copper conductors
Typical Tensile Strength Approximately 120–190kg, depending on product Some heavy-duty products can reach significantly higher tensile strengths
Electrical Resistance Higher resistance; product specification must be checked Much lower resistance on suitable long-distance products
Typical Application Shorter or portable fences Longer or more demanding permanent installations
Selection rule: Do not choose tape based only on width. For longer fences, check the manufacturer's resistance specification and select an appropriate low-resistance product where required.
