Intermediate Insulators for Horse Fencing

Intermediate insulators are installed along straight sections of an electric horse fence. Their job is to hold the conductor in the correct position while preventing the live conductor from making electrical contact with the supporting post.

They are commonly used with horse tape, polytape and electric fencing rope systems. Selecting an insulator that matches the conductor type is important for maintaining mechanical stability and reliable electrical performance.

Nexpak Security Solutions supplies equestrian fencing components suitable for paddocks, training yards, stables and private horse properties.
