What Is an Activator Plate?

An activator plate is a metal hardware component used as part of an electric-fence gate connection. It provides a secure electrical and mechanical connection point for the gate arrangement.

In equestrian fencing, the component is commonly used where a poly tape gate crosses a gate opening. The gate handle can connect to the plate or associated gate hardware while maintaining the electrical circuit when the gate is closed.

Electrical Connection Helps transfer the electric fence pulse into the gate connection.
Gate Anchor Provides a secure attachment point for an insulated gate handle.
Horse-Fence Application Designed for gate arrangements used with equestrian electric fencing.
🔌

How the Gate Connection Works

An electric fence needs electrical continuity throughout the live fencing system. A gate creates an opening in the physical fence, so suitable gate hardware is used to allow the electrical circuit to continue across the opening when the gate is closed.

JVA Activator Plates

JVA activator plates are designed specifically for electric horse-fence gate applications. They provide a secure connection point for insulated gate handles and help maintain electrical continuity through the gate system.

Standard Activator Plate A stainless-steel plate designed to work with suitable JVA corner strain components and provide an anchor point for a gate handle.
3-Way Activator Plate A multi-directional option that can accommodate several gate or fence connections from one post.
Heavy-Duty Construction Metal construction provides a durable connection point suitable for outdoor equestrian fencing applications.
Gate Handle Connection Provides a suitable attachment point for an insulated gate handle within the fence arrangement.
⚙️

Nemtek Gate Connection Options

Nemtek uses dedicated gate connection hardware for similar applications. Depending on the system design, a 3-way gate connector or suitable tape buckle can provide the electrical and mechanical connection required at the gate.

3-Way Gate Connector Allows multiple gate or fence connections to be arranged from a suitable post.
Gate Tape Connection Provides a secure connection between compatible poly tape and the gate system.
Electrical Continuity Properly installed metal connections help maintain continuity between the live fence and gate arrangement.
System Compatibility Components should be selected to match the tape, insulators, posts and other hardware used in the fence.

Correct Installation

The installation method will depend on the specific activator plate, connector, corner insulator and gate design. The following provides general best-practice guidance.

1

Switch Off the Energiser

Always switch off and isolate the electric fence energiser before working on the gate or fence.

2

Prepare the Post

Ensure the post and mounting area are structurally sound and suitable for the selected hardware.

3

Mount the Hardware

Secure the activator plate or gate connector according to the manufacturer's instructions.

4

Connect the Tape

Connect compatible horse tape and ensure that the conductive elements make proper electrical contact.

5

Attach the Gate Handle

Attach the insulated gate handle to the designated connection point and ensure that it is mechanically secure.

6

Test the Fence

Restore the system and use an appropriate electric fence tester to verify correct operation.

⚠️

Common Installation Mistakes

!
Loose Hardware A loose activator plate or connector can create an unreliable mechanical and electrical connection.
!
Poor Electrical Contact The metal component does not make reliable contact with the conductive elements of the tape.
!
Incorrect Components Hardware is used with tape or insulators for which it was not designed.
!
Damaged Tape Frayed, cut or damaged tape is left connected to the gate system.
!
Incorrect Gate Tension Excessive tension can place unnecessary stress on the tape, hardware and posts.
!
Fence Not Tested The gate connection is installed but the completed circuit is never checked with a suitable fence tester.

Safety & Best Practice

Electric fencing must be installed and maintained responsibly. Horse-fence gate systems should also be designed with animal safety and the physical behaviour of horses in mind.

  • Always switch off and isolate the energiser before working on the fence.
  • Use compatible components from reputable manufacturers.
  • Inspect gate hardware regularly for damage, corrosion or looseness.
  • Ensure gate handles and connections are properly insulated and secured.
  • Keep vegetation away from the live fence and gate connections.
  • Use a suitable electric fence tester to verify the completed system.
  • Follow the manufacturer's installation and safety instructions for each component.
