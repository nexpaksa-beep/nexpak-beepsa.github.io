What Are Gate Handles & Gate Breaks?

Electric fence gate handles and gate-break systems allow paddock and field entrances to be opened and closed while maintaining the electrical integrity of the surrounding fence.

For equestrian applications, the gate system should provide a secure mechanical connection, good electrical continuity and sufficient insulation so that the user can operate the gate safely.

High-visibility tape gates can also provide a clearly visible boundary for horses when correctly designed and installed.
