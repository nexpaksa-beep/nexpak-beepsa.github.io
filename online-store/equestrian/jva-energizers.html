JVA Energizer MB & SV Ranges

Understanding the difference between JVA mains/battery hybrid energizers and integrated solar energizers helps you select the right power source for your electric fence installation.

The JVA MB Range and JVA SV Range represent two distinct power configurations for electric fence energizers. The MB range is designed around mains and external battery power, while the SV range provides integrated solar-powered operation.

RANGE OVERVIEW

Two Power Configurations

Choose between a mains/battery hybrid solution or a self-contained solar energizer.

JVA MB Range

The MB range uses mains power or an external battery depending on the installation. These units are suited to permanent installations where higher output and flexible power options are required.

JVA SV Range

The SV range combines the energizer, solar panel and internal battery into an integrated unit. These models are designed for portable and off-grid fencing applications.

01 — MB RANGE

JVA MB Range

Mains / Battery Hybrid Energizers

The MB designation refers to the Mains/Battery configuration. Depending on the model and installation, these energizers can operate from mains power or an external battery system.

Flexible power source Designed around mains or external battery operation for permanent electric fence installations.
Solar capability Battery-based installations can be combined with an appropriate external solar charging system where required.
Permanent installations Suitable for permanent horse fencing, property boundaries, farm perimeters and other installations where a reliable, continuously powered energizer is required.
High-output performance The MB range includes models from 1.5 Joules through to 16 Joules, allowing the energizer to be selected according to fence length, vegetation load and the required operating voltage.
Monitoring on selected IP models The MB8, MB12 and MB16 IP models provide advanced monitoring capabilities, allowing fence performance and fault conditions to be monitored through compatible JVA monitoring technology.

BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Select the Energizer According to the Fence

Energizer selection should not be based only on the physical length of the fence. Fence construction, vegetation contact, conductor type, number of strands and the required operating voltage all affect the required energizer capacity.

A professional installation should also take into account grounding, insulation quality, gate arrangements and possible vegetation loading.

Information to Have Ready

  • Approximate total fence length
  • Number of electrified strands
  • Expected vegetation contact
  • Mains electricity availability
  • External battery requirements
  • Permanent or remote installation

Electric Fence Safety

Electric fencing should be designed, installed and maintained in accordance with applicable local regulations and standards. Always use appropriate warning signage and ensure that the installation is safe for people, animals and property.

Which JVA Energizer Is Right For You?

Choose MB

Choose the MB range when you have mains electricity available or already have an external battery system available for the fence.

Choose SV

Choose the SV range when the fence is remote, portable or does not have convenient access to mains electricity.

Check Fence Length

Professional Installation Matters

Energizer performance depends on the complete electric fence system. Correct earthing, suitable conductors, quality insulators, appropriate vegetation clearance and proper installation are essential for reliable operation.

Always follow the manufacturer's installation instructions and applicable South African electric fencing requirements.
