JVA Energizer MB & SV Ranges

Understanding the difference between JVA mains/battery hybrid energizers and integrated solar energizers helps you select the right power source for your electric fence installation.

The JVA MB Range and JVA SV Range represent two distinct power configurations for electric fence energizers. The MB range is designed around mains and external battery power, while the SV range provides integrated solar-powered operation.

RANGE OVERVIEW

Two Power Configurations

Choose between a mains/battery hybrid solution or a self-contained solar energizer.

JVA MB Range

The MB range uses mains power or an external battery depending on the installation. These units are suited to permanent installations where higher output and flexible power options are required.

JVA SV Range

The SV range combines the energizer, solar panel and internal battery into an integrated unit. These models are designed for portable and off-grid fencing applications.
