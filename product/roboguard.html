01 HOW IT WORKS

A Wireless Detection Layer Around Your Property

Wireless perimeter beams can be positioned at strategic points around a property to monitor defined outdoor areas. The detection field helps identify movement before an intruder reaches the building or other protected assets.

Instead of relying only on security measures installed directly on the building, perimeter detection creates an additional layer between the property boundary and the structures being protected.

SITE-SPECIFIC SECURITY SOLUTIONS
01 What is Roboguard?

Roboguard is a wireless outdoor perimeter detection solution designed to identify movement within configured areas and provide an alert to the property's security system.

02 Where can wireless beams be used?

Wireless perimeter detectors can be considered for suitable outdoor areas such as property boundaries, gardens, driveways, approaches and selected access routes. The exact placement depends on the property and site conditions.

03 Can Roboguard work with other security systems?

Depending on the equipment and system design, perimeter detection can form part of a layered security solution alongside systems such as CCTV, alarms and other security equipment.

04 Is a site assessment necessary?

A site assessment is strongly recommended for a professional installation. Terrain, vegetation, property layout, normal movement and existing security equipment can all influence the system design.

05 Can Roboguard cover a large property?

Larger properties can be designed using multiple detection points or zones where appropriate. The final configuration depends on the property layout, equipment selection and system requirements.

06 Can I buy the equipment without installation?

Equipment supply requirements can vary depending on the product and project. Contact Nexpak with your requirements so we can advise on the appropriate equipment and configuration.
