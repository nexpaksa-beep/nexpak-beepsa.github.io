The Programme

Professional knowledge. Practical thinking.

The Nexpak Advanced Electric Fencing Systems programme is designed around the complete lifecycle of an electric-fence system — from site assessment and design through installation, testing, troubleshooting and maintenance.

⚡

Engineering

Understand voltage, current, resistance, energy, pulse operation, fence loading and electrical performance.

📐

System Design

Assess properties, identify risks, design fence layouts and select appropriate components.

🔧

Installation

Study construction methods, conductors, insulators, gates, HT cable, sectioning and professional installation practices.

🧪

Testing & Diagnostics

Develop systematic testing, commissioning and advanced fault-finding skills.

Curriculum

Built around the complete electric-fencing lifecycle.

The curriculum progresses from engineering fundamentals into design, installation, testing, diagnostics, compliance and professional practice.

MODULE 01

Electric Fence Engineering Principles

Voltage, current, resistance, energy, pulses, Joules, loading, leakage and electrical performance.

MODULE 02

System Architecture

Understand the complete architecture of a professional electric-fence system.

MODULE 03

Site Assessment

Property surveying, risk identification, terrain, access, vegetation and existing infrastructure.

MODULE 04

System Design

Residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural and specialist applications.

MODULE 05

Energizers

Energizer technology, selection, output characteristics and applications.

MODULE 06

Earthing Systems

Earth electrodes, soil conditions, resistance, testing and troubleshooting.

MODULE 07

Conductors & HT Cable

Conductive materials, connections, terminations, cable routing and mechanical considerations.

MODULE 08

Fence Construction

Wall-top, free-standing, agricultural, game and specialised systems.

MODULE 09

Gates & Sectioning

Gate arrangements, continuity, isolation and access design.

MODULE 10

Testing & Commissioning

Inspection, testing, fault isolation, commissioning and documentation.

MODULE 11

Advanced Fault Finding

Systematic diagnosis of complex electric-fence faults.

MODULE 12

Lightning & Surge Protection

Environmental risks, protection, storm damage and post-storm inspection.

MODULE 13

Maintenance & Service

Preventative maintenance, inspection schedules and professional records.

MODULE 14

South African Compliance

Regulations, standards, certification and professional responsibilities.

MODULE 15

Professional Practice

Site surveys, quotations, BOMs, documentation and customer handover.

MODULE 16

Capstone Case Study

Complete a professional system design from assessment through commissioning.

MODULE 17

Advanced Security System Design & Integration

Advanced security system architecture, integration of security technologies, system planning and professional security solutions.

FINAL EXAM

Professional Security Technician Examination

Complete the comprehensive 50-question examination covering the knowledge, technical principles, installation practices and professional standards presented throughout the Academy.
