Stafix Agricultural & Wildlife Fence Architecture

While Stafix Security Centres distribute high-security perimeter equipment, the Stafix-branded agricultural energizer line is renowned worldwide for cutting-edge engineering in farm, equestrian, and wildlife fence management. Defining hallmarks include Cyclic Wave™ technology (delivering clean, continuous pulses right to the end of long wire loops) and Bi-polar technology, guaranteeing high shocking performance even in bone-dry, non-conductive soils.

Core Stafix Product Tiers & Capabilities

  • X-Series Unigizers (Cross-Over Range): Versatile dual-power units running on either 220V mains or 12V deep-cycle batteries. Models range from the entry-level X1 (1.0J) to heavy-duty X18i (18 Joules) featuring isolated digital display screens monitoring real-time output voltage and battery health.
  • High-Power Mains Series (Massive Perimeters): Built for multi-kilometer cattle grids, massive game reserves, and wild animal boundaries. The flagship Stafix M46 replaces the legacy M36R, offering 20% more power, built-in Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, and full integration with the Datamars Livestock Farm App for smartphone monitoring and remote power control.
  • Portable & Battery-Powered Strip Grazers: Lightweight, compact units (AN20, AN90) designed for rotational strip grazing, temporary cell division, and horse show camps, with options for clip-on solar panels to create autonomous remote stations.

Professional Farm Deployment Methodology

  1. Grounding Infrastructure: Agricultural systems rely heavily on earth return. Drive multiple galvanized earth stakes deep into damp soil away from dairy or structural earth paths to ensure maximum energy transfer.
  2. Vegetation Management & Cyclic Wave: Use Cyclic Wave™ energizers to push past light foliage, but routinely clear high-growth brush along fence lines to prevent voltage drop and power leakage.
  3. Smart App & Remote Telemetry: Leverage Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled mains energizers (like the M46) to inspect farm boundary status and isolate faults instantly via mobile devices across vast acreage.

Agricultural Grounding Tip

In dry sandy or arid soils, use a Bi-polar wiring configuration and alternate live and earth wires to guarantee an effective shock when animals make contact with the fence.
