6-Line Perimeter Protection Systems

Engineered for high-risk residential and commercial perimeters, the NexPak 6-Line configuration is built around the intelligent JVA Z14 Energizer platform. It provides active high-voltage deterrence paired with real-time zone monitoring and compliance readiness.

South African Legal & Regulatory Framework

In terms of the Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA) and the Electrical Machinery Regulations governed by SANS 10222-3, all electric fence installations in South Africa must meet uncompromising statutory requirements:

  • Mandatory Certificate of Compliance (CoC): Every new installation requires a valid CoC issued by a registered electric fence installer. This document is legally required for property transfer or insurance compliance.
  • Warning Signage Interval Regulations: High-visibility yellow warning signs ("CAUTION - ELECTRIC FENCE") must be securely displayed at all gates, access points, and at intervals not exceeding 10 meters along public thoroughfares.
  • Physical Height Clearances: Brackets must maintain legal vertical or angled clearances over pedestrian pathways (minimum 2.2 meters from walking surfaces if uninsulated or accessible).
  • Independent Earthing Grid: The system must feature dedicated copper-plated earth spikes coupled with high-voltage cable, completely separate from the primary household electrical earth.

Professional Installation & Training Guide

Achieving system reliability and eliminating false triggers requires precise mechanical and electrical execution:

  1. Bracket Layout & Alignment: Space 6-line square tube brackets at maximum 3-meter intervals. Reinforce all corners, end-posts, and gate pillars using 750mm stays and stay sleeves to counteract high wire tension loads.
  2. Solid Anchoring: Fasten brackets securely into concrete or brick structures using 8x80mm nail-in anchors. Ensure zero structural flex under extreme wind conditions.
  3. Wire Stringing & Termination: Thread the 1.2mm braided galvanized wire smoothly through the lugs. Maintain correct line tension using tweaker combos and S-hooks, crimping ends tightly with 6mm ferrules to prevent slippage.
  4. Surge & Lightning Mitigation: Install a Dual Lightning Divertor at the perimeter entry point before the energizer, bonded directly to dedicated copper-plated earth spikes to safeguard against electrostatic discharge and lightning surges.
  5. Power & Accessory Integration: Wire the 16 VA PSU, 7 Amp backup battery, 15W siren, red strobe, nite light, and single gate contact. Configure the JVA Z14 parameters for gate delay and alarm response thresholds.

Installer Best Practice

Always verify loop resistance and conduct an end-to-end Joule output sweep using a digital fence scope before issuing the final CoC documentation and client handover.
