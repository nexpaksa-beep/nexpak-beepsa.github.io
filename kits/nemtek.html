Nemtek Electric Fencing Architecture

The Nemtek range of security energizers forms the benchmark for perimeter electric fencing across residential, commercial, and high-security infrastructure. Featuring proprietary Adaptive Power Technology (APT), Nemtek systems automatically adjust output power to account for changing weather or vegetation growth, minimizing false alarms and preventing power wastage.

South African Legal & Statutory Safety Compliance (SANS 10222-3)

Electric fence installations in South Africa are heavily legislated under the Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA) and SANS 10222-3 safety standards:

  • Certificate of Compliance (COC): A legal COC issued by a registered electric fence installer is mandatory upon completion or property transfer. Operating an uncertified fence carries severe liability risks.
  • Warning Signage: Weather-resistant warning signs ("ELECTRIC FENCE") must be prominently displayed on all access gates, boundaries, and at intervals not exceeding 10 meters along public thoroughfares.
  • Height & Proximity Regulations: Wall-top brackets must maintain prescribed minimum clearances from public walkways and adjacent overhead electrical lines. Barbed wire or razor wire must not be electrified.
  • Earthing Infrastructure: Proper low-impedance copper earth spikes must be driven deep into moist soil at specified intervals to ensure rapid trip-sensing and high-voltage discharge safety.

Professional Installation & Range Selection Methodology

Selecting and deploying the correct Nemtek energizer family depends on property scope and monitoring needs:

  1. Wizord Range (Residential Workhorse): Features rugged simplicity and colored LED status indicators (Wizord 2i, 3i, and 4i models ranging from 2J to 3.7J) with built-in battery backup and optional Nemtek Connect integration.
  2. Druid Range (LCD Single & Dual Zone): Displays exact return kilovolts on a liquid crystal display. Single-zone (13LCD to 18LCD) and independent dual-zone models (25LCD, 28LCD) allow precise breach identification across separate property perimeters.
  3. Merlin & Merlin Stealth (Commercial & Networked): Engineered for industrial sites and estates, supporting master/slave networking, multi-zone expandability, and dedicated remote keypads (Merlin 4i, M18S, M28S).

Professional Commissioning Tip

Always verify earth loop resistance and integrate the energizer with a backup deep-cycle battery and lightning protection choke kit to prevent surge damage during severe summer thunderstorms.
