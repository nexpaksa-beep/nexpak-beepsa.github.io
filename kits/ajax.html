Ajax Wireless Alarm Ecosystem & Radio Architecture

Ajax wireless alarms are widely considered the gold standard for modern, smartphone-controlled smart home and commercial security. Designed with a sleek, minimalist aesthetic, Ajax operates on Jeweller and Wings radio technology—proprietary encrypted wireless protocols that deliver a massive operational range of up to 2,000 metres line-of-sight while consuming minimal battery power. Every device in the Ajax ecosystem is fully supervised, meaning the central hub pings sensors every 12 to 36 seconds to instantly detect signal jamming or tampering.

Core Hub Architecture & Communication Channels

  • Ajax Hub 2 (2G/4G): Standard residential benchmark supporting up to 100 wireless devices, 25 cameras, and photo verification via MotionCam sensors.
  • Ajax Hub 2 Plus: Engineered for high-security sites supporting up to 150 devices and four simultaneous communication paths (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and two independent 4G SIM slots).
  • Ajax Hub Hybrid: Revolutionary crossover panel supporting 100 wireless endpoints alongside Fibra wired loops, allowing installers to reuse legacy cabling.

Professional Integration & Automation (Smart Scenarios)

  1. Rapid Photo Verification: Delivers rich photo verification push notifications within 9 seconds of a zone breach to confirm intruder validity.
  2. Smart Scenarios: Automate lighting triggers upon outdoor beam activation or shut off main water valves via LeaksProtect sensors upon pipe bursts.
  3. Armed Response Control Room Linkage: Fully compatible with local armed response station monitoring protocols over SIA/Contact ID via IP or cellular channels.

Professional Commissioning Tip

Always configure dual-path internet redundancy (Ethernet + LTE SIM) on Hub 2 Plus installations and test Jeweller signal attenuation during site survey commissioning.
