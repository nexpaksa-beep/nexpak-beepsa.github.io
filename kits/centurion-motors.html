Centurion's cutting-edge Smart range—encompassing the D3 Smart, D5 Smart, D6 Smart, D10 Smart / D10 Smart Turbo, Vantage 500, and SDO Smart—combines high-speed industrial microprocessors, ultra-reliable battery backup architectures, and advanced IoT integration via the MyCentaG platform.

South African Legal & Safety Compliance Framework

Automated gate and garage door systems installed in South Africa are legally governed by rigorous statutory standards under the Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA), the National Building Regulations, and machine safety alignments such as EN 12453 / SANS codes:

  • Mandatory Obstruction Safety Beams: Automated gates must incorporate functional infrared safety beams (photocells) to prevent crushing or entrapment hazards during closing cycles. Omitting compliant beams violates safety liabilities and insurance rules.
  • Electronic Collision Sensing: Centurion Smart controllers feature onboard collision-detection algorithms. Installers must calibrate obstacle force thresholds to ensure the gate immediately reverses upon encountering resistance.
  • Emergency Manual Override: A secure, operational manual release mechanism must be accessible to allow manual operation during total power failure or internal fault states.
  • Electrical Safety Isolation: Mains supply wiring supplying the charging transformer must conform to SANS 10142-1 wiring codes, terminating via an accessible double-pole isolator switch.

Professional Installation & Training Training Methodology

Achieving silent, high-speed, and trouble-free operation requires precise mechanical preparation and setup:

  1. Baseplate & Foundation Leveling: Pour a vibration-free, reinforced concrete foundation. Level the baseplate perfectly; incorrect leveling causes gear binding, high motor current draw, and rapid wear.
  2. Rack-to-Pinion Clearance (Sliding Kits): Mount nylon or steel racks maintaining a precise 2mm to 3mm clearance above the pinion gear. Never allow the static weight of the gate to rest on the motor pinion.
  3. Limit Switch & Origin Setup: Position magnetic origin limits accurately to trigger smooth electronic soft-stop deceleration curves before full closure or opening limits.
  4. Actuator Geometry (Vantage 500): Measure mounting brackets meticulously using Centurion's geometric installation charts to ensure correct leverage and smooth travel across the swing arc without hinge binding.
  5. Shaft & Rail Setup (SDO Smart Garage): Secure the garage operator shaft and rail assembly properly to ceiling trusses, ensuring balanced tension on the sectional or tip-up door springs.

Professional Commissioning Tip

Always perform a complete auto-set calibration routine via the MyCentaG mobile app after installation. This maps out gate limits, obstruction forces, and battery charging profiles automatically.
