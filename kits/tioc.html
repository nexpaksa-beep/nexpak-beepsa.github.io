Dahua TiOC Architecture & Active Deterrence Ecosystem

Dahua TiOC (Three-in-One Camera) security cameras are premium, pro-grade surveillance units engineered for proactive crime prevention rather than passive recording. The "Three-in-One" name comes from the seamless integration of three distinct technologies into a single hardware housing: full-color 24/7 imaging, WizSense AI analytics, and active police-style deterrence strobe lights and audio sirens.

Core Integrated Technologies

  • 24/7 Full-Colour Imaging: Employs ultra-wide F1.0 apertures and built-in warm white LEDs to capture vibrant color video in pitch-black conditions.
  • WizSense AI Analytics: Powered by deep-learning chips running Smart Motion Detection (SMD 4.0) to accurately classify human and vehicle targets, reducing false alarms from rain, foliage, or animals to under 2%.
  • Active Deterrence: Immediately fires flashing red and blue strobe lights and customizable audio sirens the moment a real human or vehicle crosses a digital tripwire.

Product Generations & Form Factors

  1. TiOC 2.0 Series: Introduces Smart Dual Light Technology (recording via discreet IR and switching to warm light upon intrusion to reduce light pollution) and VoiceCatcher dual-microphone noise filtering.
  2. TiOC 3.0 Series: Upgrades AI processors, speaker clarity for two-way talk, scheduled arming profiles, and adjustable siren volume controls.
  3. TiOC PRO & Duo Series: Flagship hardware utilizing massive 1/1.8" image sensors, WizColour low-light ISP optimization, AcuPick search compatibility, and 180-degree dual-lens panoramic coverage.

Mobile Ecosystem & One-Tap Disarm

Seamlessly integrated with the Dahua DMSS Mobile App. Push notifications deliver instant video clips upon intrusion, while the One-Tap Disarm button lets you temporarily silence sirens and lights during garden work or social gatherings without missing regular background recordings.
