Frequently Asked Questions About Safety Hard Hats

What does SANS certification mean?

SANS (South African National Standards) certification ensures the hard hat meets rigorous safety standards for impact resistance, durability, and protection. SANS certified hard hats provide legally compliant head protection for South African workplaces.

How long do hard hats last?

With proper care, a hard hat typically lasts 3-5 years. However, replace immediately after any significant impact or if visible damage occurs. Store in a cool, dry location away from direct sunlight.

Are chin straps included?

Chin straps are available but sold separately. They're recommended for overhead work or windy conditions to prevent the hard hat from falling off.
