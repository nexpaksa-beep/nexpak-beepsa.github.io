CCTV Frequently Asked Questions

What CCTV system do I need?

The correct system depends on property size, camera locations, monitoring requirements and recording needs.

Can CCTV cameras be viewed remotely?

Yes. Modern CCTV systems support remote viewing through smartphones, tablets and computers.

What is the difference between DVR and NVR?

DVR systems are commonly used with analog cameras, while NVR systems are designed for IP network cameras.
