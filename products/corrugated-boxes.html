Frequently Asked Questions About Corrugated Boxes

What's the difference between single, double, and triple wall boxes?

Single wall has one layer of fluting - best for lightweight items under 5kg. Double wall has two fluting layers - ideal for general purpose packaging of items up to 20kg. Triple wall has three layers - for heavy items over 20kg or multiple stacking.

How should I store empty corrugated boxes?

Store in a dry location away from moisture and direct sunlight. Keep in cool temperatures (18-24°C ideal). Properly stacked, they stack efficiently and remain strong and rigid.

Yes! Our corrugated cardboard is 100% recyclable. It's an eco-friendly packaging solution and customers appreciate the sustainability aspect.
