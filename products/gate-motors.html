Gate Automation FAQ

Which gate motor do I need?

The correct motor depends on gate type, gate weight, usage frequency and security requirements.

Can gate motors work during power outages?

Yes. Battery backup and solar options can keep automated gates operating during power interruptions.

Can gate automation connect to access control?

Yes. Gate motors can integrate with intercoms, keypads, GSM modules and access control systems.
