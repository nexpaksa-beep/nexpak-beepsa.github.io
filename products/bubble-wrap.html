Frequently Asked Questions About Bubble Wrap

What makes bubble wrap effective for protection?

Bubble wrap works through air-cushioned bubbles that absorb impact, shock, and vibration. When items bump or drop, the air cushions compress instead of transferring force to the packaged product, preventing damage.

What bubble size should I choose?

Use small bubbles (10mm) for lightweight fragile items like glassware. Medium bubbles (25mm) for general packaging. Large bubbles (50mm) for heavier items and maximum impact protection. We recommend consulting our team for specific applications.

Is bubble wrap environmentally friendly?

Our bubble wrap is made from recyclable polyethylene. It can be recycled through most plastic recycling programs in South Africa. Many customers reuse it multiple times before recycling.

What's the difference between single and multi-bubble wrap?

Single bubble wrap has one layer of bubbles. Multi-bubble wrap has multiple bubble layers for enhanced protection. Multi-bubble is ideal for valuable or fragile items requiring maximum protection.
