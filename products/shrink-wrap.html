Frequently Asked Questions About Shrink Wrap

What temperature should I use for shrinking?

Typical heat gun temperature is 100-120°C. Keep the gun 15-20cm away from the film. Different equipment may require slight temperature adjustments. Always test on sample products first.

What equipment do I need to apply shrink wrap?

A heat shrink machine (tunnel shrink, heat gun, or L-bar sealer) is recommended for professional operations. Manual application with a heat gun is possible for smaller volumes but less efficient.

Can I use shrink wrap for irregular-shaped products?

Yes! Shrink wrap conforms to most shapes when heated. However, very sharp edges or fragile items may need protective padding between the product and the film.

Is shrink wrap food-safe?

Our shrink wrap is suitable for non-perishable food items. For perishable foods or direct food contact, confirm food-grade certification before use. We can provide food-grade options on request.

What's the shelf life of shrink wrap film?

Properly stored shrink wrap typically lasts 2-3 years. Store in cool, dry conditions away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures.

Can I print on the shrink wrap?

Yes, we can arrange pre-printed shrink wrap with your logo or product information for bulk orders. Contact us for custom printing options.
