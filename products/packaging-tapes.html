Frequently Asked Questions About Packaging Tape

What's the best width for general box sealing?

48mm is ideal for most standard boxes as it provides fast, secure sealing. 36mm is good for smaller boxes, and 24mm for lightweight packages. Wider tape covers more area with fewer applications.

Should I use brown or clear tape?

Brown tape is economical and professional for general shipping. Clear tape is better when you want the box design or contents to remain visible through the sealed edges.

How long does sealed tape last?

With our weather-resistant formula, sealed packages remain secure for months, even in storage or changing conditions. The seal remains effective during transport and storage.

Can I use this tape on wet boxes?

No, boxes should be dry before sealing. Our weather-resistant formula protects against moisture after sealing, but initial application requires dry surfaces for optimal adhesion.

Is this tape suitable for freezer storage?

Yes! Our tape remains effective at low temperatures. For extended cold storage, our freezer-grade tape (sold separately) is specially formulated for extreme cold.

Do you offer bulk contracts for shipping companies?

Yes! We provide volume pricing and contract terms for logistics and e-commerce companies with ongoing tape needs. Contact us for wholesale rates.

What's the best application technique?

Use a tape dispenser for fast, consistent application. Apply tape to all seams - both the center seam and along the top and bottom edges of the box. Use hand pressure for optimal adhesion.
