IP CCTV Frequently Asked Questions

What is the advantage of IP CCTV?

IP CCTV systems offer higher image quality, network connectivity, remote viewing and advanced AI features compared to traditional analogue systems.

Can I view my cameras on my phone?

Yes. IP CCTV systems support secure remote viewing through compatible mobile applications and web platforms.

Do IP cameras require internet?

No. IP cameras operate on your local network. Internet access is only required if you want to view your cameras remotely.
